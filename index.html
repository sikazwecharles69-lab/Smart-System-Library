Welcome to SmartLibrary

SmartLibrary is a proposed school library management system designed to make book management, borrowing, returning, and availability tracking easier and more efficient.

Explore SmartLibrary

The Problem

The school library currently uses manual records to manage books. This can make it difficult to know which books are available, who has borrowed a book, and when it should be returned.

Who is affected?

  • Students
  • Librarians
  • Teachers
  • School Administration

Our Proposed System

SmartLibrary

SmartLibrary is designed to provide an organized digital way to manage books, borrowing, returning, and book availability.

Intended Users

  • Librarians
  • Students
  • Lecture
  • Administrators

Features & Benefits

Book Management

Librarians can add, edit, delete, and search for books.

Borrowing & Returning

The system records borrowed books, borrowing dates, and expected return dates.

Book Availability Search

Students and staff can search for books and see whether they are available or already borrowed.
