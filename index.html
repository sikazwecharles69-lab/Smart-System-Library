The Problem

Manual library records slow everything down.

The school library currently relies on manual record keeping, which makes it difficult to know which books are available, who has borrowed a title, and when it should be returned.

This creates delays, misplaced records, and poor visibility for students, librarians, and staff.

Who is affected?

  • Students
  • Librarians
  • Teachers
  • School administration

Proposed solution

Our proposed system

SmartLibrary

SmartLibrary is designed to provide an organized digital way to manage books, borrowing, returning, and book availability.

Intended users

  • Librarians
  • Students
  • Lecturers
  • Administrators
